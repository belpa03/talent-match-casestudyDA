## 📊 INSIGHT 3: Month-to-Month Patterns

**VISUAL:** `charts_pro/02_monthly_deep_dive.png`

### 📊 WHAT WE SAW

**Performance Trajectory:**
- Q2-Q3 2024: Stable zone (60-70 tasks consistently)
- Q4 2024: Slight dip but healthy (55-59 tasks)
- Q1 2025: Recovery (30-40 tasks)
- Q3 2025: Surge to peak (101 tasks)
- Q4 2025: Crash (35 tasks)

**Pattern Recognition:**
- Most stable period: Jul-Oct 2024 (CV = 7%)
- Most volatile: Nov-Dec 2025 (CV = 47%)
- Month-over-month changes: -50 to +30 tasks

### 🔍 KEY FINDINGS

| Pattern | Observation | Analysis |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Seasonal Signal** | Q2-Q3 strong, Q4 weak | Possible holiday impact OR resource cycling |
| **Recent Anomaly** | Nov peak then Dec crash | Unnatural pattern - suggests one-time event/deadline |
| **Baseline Erosion** | Average declining 2024→2025 | Long-term productivity trend negative |
| **Predictability Loss** | Recent 3 months: +32, +0, -66 | Cannot forecast - operational risk |

### 💡 CONCLUSION
**Team lost operational rhythm.** Historical data shows capability for consistent 65-70 task months (Jul-Oct 2024 benchmark). November spike to 101 appears to be deadline-driven sprint followed by burnout/recovery in December. 

**True sustainable capacity** likely 60-70 tasks/month based on most stable historical period.

### 🎯 CRITICAL ACTION
**P1 - This Month:**
1. Investigate Nov 2025 spike - was it sustainable or burnout-inducing?
2. Set realistic targets: 60 tasks (conservative), 75 tasks (stretch)
3. Implement weekly tracking to catch variances early
4. Identify and address Q4 seasonality factors
